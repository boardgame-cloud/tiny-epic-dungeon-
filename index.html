Game Setup

Setup for 4 Heroes. Solo: control at least 2 Heroes. The number of Heroes is the player count.

Separate the Entrance, Rooms, Encounters, and Lair Door. Shuffle each deck.

  1. A
    Deck A: draw 5 Rooms.
  2. B
    Deck B: draw 4 Encounters and 12 Rooms. Shuffle together unseen.
  3. C
    Deck C: draw 3 Rooms and shuffle with the Lair Door.
  4. D
    Stack facedown: C bottom, B middle, A top.
  5. E
    Return unused Rooms and Encounters to the box.

The Dungeon

  2. G

  3. H

  4. I

Dungeon Size Limit: no card more than 3 cards from the Entrance in any direction (a 7x7 grid).

On Your Turn

In any order, all optional:

MoveUp to your Speed. A Heroic Action ends movement.
One Heroic ActionRest, Search, Disarm, Cast a Spell, or Attack.
Any Free ActionsAnytime on your turn, even mid-move.
End of TurnTorch must move down 1. Resolve its space.
  • Health: below 1, you fall unconscious.
  • Focus: pays for abilities and die changes. Zero has no penalty.
  • Speed: max rooms you may move into.
  • Defense: damage negated from a Counter-Attack.

Strength Agility Intellect = Hero Dice rolled. Never more than 3, never fewer than 1.

Move

Spend 1 Speed per room entered, along pathways (connected corridors).

  • Enemies: entering their room ends your movement, unless you have Stealth.
  • Heroes: never block movement. Any number of Heroes and enemies may share a room.
  • Traps: evasion check on every entry unless disarmed. See Traps.

Revealing New Rooms

  1. Spend 1 Speed to flip an adjacent facedown card.
  2. Rotate it so a pathway connects to your room.
  3. Place facedown cards at every open corridor leading to an empty grid space (skip if the deck is empty).
  4. You must move in (unless a Room-icon ability says otherwise).
  5. Check for Danger. Then keep moving if able.

Check for Danger

Goblin EncounterTake 1 damage (if in the room); movement ends. Draw a Goblin Card into the lowest empty slot; place its token here. A fifth Goblin = you lose.
Minion EncounterTake 2 damage (if in the room); movement ends. Draw a Minion Card (right of the Torch Mat), add an Enemy Health Marker at its top, place its token here.
TrapEvasion check on each entry. See Traps.

Skill Checks

Roll your Skill's dice and meet or exceed the number. Green check = success, red X = failure (none listed, no effect). A cost on the success box must be paid to get the effect.

  1. Select one die as the result.
  2. Plus dice (1, 2) may add to the result die, or be the result.
  3. Apply modifiers.
  4. Optional Free Action: 2 Focus changes one Hero Die by 1 (no 6↔1, not the Enemy Die).
  5. On success only: unused dice give +1 Focus per Focus icon, +1 Health per 6.

Heroic Actions

One per turn. Ends your movement.

Heroic Rest

+3 Health, +5 Focus. Mandatory if you start unconscious.

Heroic Search a Room

Use the room's feature (check or cost). Not with enemies present unless another conscious Hero is there.

Heroic Disarm a Trap

After the evasion check, roll the right-side check. See Traps.

Heroic Cast a Spell

Target in range, meet the check. Focus from unused dice comes first and can pay the cost. Spell damage ignores Defense.

Heroic Attack

  • Melee: same room.
  • Missile: another room, line-of-sight, in range.
  • Spell: the spell's range.

See Attack.

Range

Same RoomYour room only.
Line-of-SightStraight line of pathways, within range. Never your room.
Any PathAlong pathways within range, including your room.
AllEveryone within range along pathways, including your room.

Note: Heroes and enemies never block attacks.

Attack

  1. Target in same room or line-of-sight: also roll the Enemy Die.
  2. Melee/Missile: result must be higher than Defense. Damage = result − Defense. Equal = miss.
  3. Survivor in same room or line-of-sight Counter-Attacks.

For example: Minotaur Defense 4, +2 vs Melee = 6. You roll 8: 2 damage.

Kill Enemies

  • Goblin (1 Health): token to the Torch Mat, card discarded. Take its reward ("/" = choose one, "+" = both).
  • Minion: token and card to the box. Its Health Marker goes on a Boss Mat heart. Take 1 Loot or Spell twice. Torch up 6 (not past start).

Unconscious Hero

At 0 Health your turn ends; lay your miniature down. Next turn you must Rest, may then move, and no Free Actions. Healing from another Hero wakes you immediately.

Free Actions

Any number, anytime on your turn only, even mid-move or after rolling.

Free Modify a Hero Die

2 Focus: change a Hero Die by 1.

Free Teleport

2 Focus: to any revealed room, then keep moving. Not if an enemy stopped you. Lair Door yes, Boss Mat no.

Free Reveal Adjacent Rooms

Act 1. Torch down 1: reveal all adjacent unexplored rooms. Enemies placed, no encounter damage.

Free Research

4 Focus: discover a Spell. Unusable while a Hand Loot covers it.

Free Enter the Boss's Lair

On the Lair Door once all Minions are dead. Still costs 1 Speed. See Act 2.

Free Taunt the Boss

Act 2. 2 Focus: Boss moves up to its Speed toward you. Repeatable.

Free Discard

Any Loot or Spell to its discard row. No trading.

Trigger Action plaque + colon

Usable only while performing that action.

Traps

Left side: evasion. Right side: disarm. View Trap Cards

  1. Every entry without a Disarm Marker: roll the evasion check.
  2. Fail: take the damage in the X box. Success: no effect.
  3. Either way: keep moving, or Disarm (Heroic Action).
  4. Disarm needs no enemies present, unless another conscious Hero is there.
  5. Disarm success: place a Disarm Marker, discover 1 Loot or Spell. Ignored for the rest of the game. Fail: no effect.

Note: Moonblade's Lockpick (2 Focus) is a Free Action Disarm. Enemies ignore traps.

Torch Mat

Torch reaches the skull: you lose. It never moves up past its start (4). Moving down onto or past an icon triggers it:

Add a Goblin to the Entrance

Lowest-numbered Goblin Token to the Entrance; draw its Goblin Card. Fifth Goblin = you lose.

Trigger Enemy Actions

  1. Goblins, 1 to 4.
  2. Minions left to right (Act 1), or the Boss (Act 2).

Hits all conscious Heroes in range. Defense and abilities do not block it.

Move or AttackHero in range: attack, no move. Otherwise: move, no attack.
Move and AttackNearest Hero in its room: attack. Otherwise: move, then attack.

Enemy Movement

Toward the closest conscious Hero by the shortest pathway, up to its Speed, stopping in the first room with a conscious Hero. Ignores traps and enemies, not walls. Active player breaks ties.

Act 2: The Boss

Lair Door revealed and all 4 Minions dead: a Hero on the Lair Door may Enter the Boss's Lair. View Boss Mats

  1. Flip the Boss Mat to Act 2. Stack the 4 Health Markers on 8.
  2. Boss Token on the Boss Mat.
  3. Flip the Torch Mat to Act 2; torch to the top space for 4. Keep Goblins aligned.
  4. Altar Marker on every Minion Encounter Card.

The Boss Mat

  • One large room: same room and line-of-sight for everyone on it.
  • 1 Speed from the Lair Door onto the entrance space, then move between Hero Spaces.
  • No sharing a space at the end of movement.
  • Space effects trigger when you end movement there, not when starting a turn there.
  • Goblins can enter (center of the mat) and attack all Heroes there.

Boss Health and Altars

  • Split damage freely across the Health Markers. All at 0 = victory.
  • Markers can't reach or pass an uncovered Altar space; excess damage is lost.
  • Boss enters a room with an Altar Marker: move it to the highest uncovered Altar space.
  • Taunt the Boss to pull it to altars. With no Heroes on the Boss Mat, its Enemy Action moves it out to the nearest Hero.

Lair Rules

  • On the Boss Mat: Attack, Cast a Spell, or Rest.
  • Boss in Lair: hits only Heroes on the Boss Mat; attackable only from the Boss Mat.
  • Boss in dungeon: attack it like any enemy.
  • Boss Torch: torch down 1 + its Torch effect. Resolve the Torch effect before any Enemy Action it triggers.
  • Enemy Actions: Goblins first, then the Boss.

Loot and Spells

  • Discover (full-color icon): draw from the deck (keep or discard), or take one from the discard row.
  • Pick Up (gray icon, green arrow): discard row only.
  • Equip only when discovering or picking up.
